How to Play

Every 24 hours, a new collaborative board is generated.

You get one daily piece to place on the board. Work with other Redditors to build a Rube Goldberg path!

Tap "Simulate" to drop marbles and score points when they hit colored goal zones.

Only your highest single run score is submitted to the leaderboard.
